# <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/oliver-ponder/">Oliver Ponder</a>'s IT and Cybersecurity Project Portfolio 🔐

I'm a cybersecurity professional focused on Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC), currently supporting the Assessment & Authorization (A&A) process for VA information systems as a Risk Management Technician. My work centers on evaluating information systems and networks against IA controls, identifying vulnerabilities and security requirements in accordance with the VA's A&A process, and helping systems achieve and maintain Authorization to Operate (ATO). I hold a B.S. in Computer Science from the University of North Texas and am certified in CompTIA Security+, CySA+, and PenTest+, (ISC)² CC, and AWS Solutions Architect, which gives me a technical foundation that I bring directly into risk assessment and compliance work.

Coming from a background in software development, I'm especially interested in where GRC and agentic AI intersect, and I've built two projects that reflect that. The first is an autonomous SOC 2 Type II compliance engine that evaluates eleven controls across the Trust Services Criteria, enforces least-privilege policy decisions in real time, and records every decision to a SHA-256 cryptographically chained evidence ledger, so that compliance is proven through verifiable audit trails rather than asserted through manual attestation. The second is an Agentic AI Security Mentor platform that simulates SOC incident triage and teaches engineers to recognize agentic-specific threats such as indirect prompt injection, excessive agency, and RAG/vector store poisoning, mapped to the OWASP Agentic Top 10. Building both taught me that the next generation of GRC work will require professionals who understand traditional frameworks like NIST RMF and SOC 2 as well as the new risks introduced by autonomous AI systems.

I'm confident that combining a solid compliance foundation with hands-on experience building agentic AI tools positions me well for GRC roles that are evolving alongside AI adoption. I'm always glad to connect with others working at the intersection of cybersecurity, compliance, and AI, and I welcome the opportunity to bring this perspective to a team.
